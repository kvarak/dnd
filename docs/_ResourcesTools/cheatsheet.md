## Your Turn

*In any order, one of each (unless other rules allow).*

- <span class="tag">Move</span> up to your Speed
- <span class="tag">Action</span> one action
- <span class="tag">Bonus Action</span> one bonus action (if available)
- <span class="tag">Free</span> interact with one object

*You can split your move before/after your other actions.*

</div>
<div class="box" markdown="1">

## Surprise
If surprised: cannot move or act on your first turn; cannot take reactions until you have acted.

</div>
<div class="box" markdown="1">

## Actions in Combat

- <span class="tag">Attack</span> one attack (extra attacks may apply)
- <span class="tag">Dash</span> gain extra movement equal to your speed
- <span class="tag">Disengage</span> movement doesn't provoke OAs this turn
- <span class="tag">Dodge</span> attacks vs you have disadv.; Dex saves with adv.. Lose if incapacitated or speed 0
- <span class="tag">Help</span> give adv. on next ability check or attack roll against a creature within 5 ft
- <span class="tag">Ready</span> declare trigger + action; release as a reaction
- <span class="tag">Ready a Spell</span> cast with 1-action time; hold with concentration; release on trigger
- <span class="tag">Use an Object</span> interact with a second object
- <span class="tag">Grapple</span> Physique check vs target's Physique or Acrobatics; grappler can drag/carry at ½ speed
- <span class="tag">Shove</span> Physique vs Strength save - push 5 ft or knock prone
- <span class="tag">Escape Grapple</span> Physique or Acrobatics vs grappler's Physique

</div>
<div class="box" markdown="1">

## Opportunity Attacks
Triggered when a *visible* enemy **leaves your reach** without Disengaging. Use a <span class="tag">reaction</span> to make a melee attack.

*You have **one reaction** per round; it resets at the start of your turn.*

</div>
<div class="box" markdown="1">

## Two-Weapon Fighting
When you attack with a **light melee weapon**, use a <span class="tag">bonus action</span> to attack with a different light melee weapon in your other hand. No ability modifier to damage on the bonus attack (unless negative).

</div>

</div>

<!-- ----------------------- Column 2 ----------------------- -->

<div>

<div class="box" markdown="1">

## Initiative
Roll d20 + **Intelligence** modifier (not Dexterity). Ties broken randomly.

</div>
<div class="box" markdown="1">

## Attack Rolls
- <span class="tag">Hit</span> d20 + proficiency + ability mod ≥ target AC
- <span class="tag">Critical Hit</span> natural 20 - roll 1d100 on crit table
- <span class="tag">Fumble</span> natural 1 - roll 1d100 on fumble table
- <span class="tag">Advantage</span> roll 2d20, take higher
- <span class="tag">Disadvantage</span> roll 2d20, take lower

- <span class="tag">Melee</span> STR mod (or DEX with finesse)
- <span class="tag">Thrown</span> STR mod (or DEX with finesse)
- <span class="tag">Ranged</span> DEX mod. Disadv. in melee
- <span class="tag">Spell</span> d20 + spellcasting mod + proficiency vs AC
- <span class="tag">Range X/Y</span> normal (X ft) = no penalty; beyond X = disadv.; beyond Y = can't attack

</div>
<div class="box" markdown="1">

## Mounted Combat
- <span class="tag">Mount/dismount</span> ½ speed
- Forced move of mount → DC 10 Dex save or fall off the mount
- If mount is knocked prone: reaction to dismount safely
- <span class="tag">Controlled mount</span> on your initiative; only Dash, Disengage, Dodge
- <span class="tag">Independent mount</span> acts on its own initiative; full actions
- OA against you while mounted can target you or mount

*With* <span class="tag">Mounted Combat (trait)</span>*:*
- Advantage on melee attacks vs smaller unmounted creatures
- Can redirect attacks targeting your mount to yourself instead
- Mount takes no damage on a successful Dex save (half on fail)
</div>
<div class="box" markdown="1">

## Cover

- <span class="tag">Half cover</span> +2 AC & Dex saves
- <span class="tag">¾ cover</span> +5 AC & Dex saves
- <span class="tag">Total cover</span> can't be targeted

</div>
<div class="box" markdown="1">

## Movement
- <span class="tag">Difficult terrain</span> costs 1 extra ft per ft
- <span class="tag">Drop prone</span> free (no movement cost)
- <span class="tag">Stand up</span> costs ½ speed
- <span class="tag">Crawl</span> (while prone) - 1 extra ft per ft
- <span class="tag">Climb / Swim</span> 1 extra ft per ft (unless speed applies)
- <span class="tag">Long jump</span> STR score in ft (10 ft run-up); half standing
- <span class="tag">High jump</span> 3 + STR mod ft (10 ft run-up); half standing
- <span class="tag">Jump landing</span> in difficult terrain: DC 10 Dex (Acrobatics) or fall prone

</div>

</div>

<!-- ----------------------- Column 3 ----------------------- -->

<div>

<div class="box" markdown="1">

## Dropping to 0 HP
- <span class="tag">Instant death</span> if remaining damage ≥ your HP maximum.
- <span class="tag">Death saves</span> d20 at start of your turn. 3 successes = stable. 3 failures = dead. Natural 20 = regain 1 HP.
  - Remaining dmg > CON score → first save at **disadv.**
  - Remaining dmg ≤ CON bonus (or 0) → first save at **adv.**

- <span class="tag">Stabilize</span> DC 10 Wis (Medicine) check as action; or Healer's kit (auto-stabilize, no check).
- <span class="tag">Combat Exhaustion</span> (on return from 0 HP): gain 6 levels. Lose 1 at the start of each round. Reaching 6+ = <span class="tag">Stunned</span> (not dead).

</div>
<div class="box" markdown="1">

## Spellcasting in Combat
- <span class="tag">Bonus action spell</span> you may only cast a cantrip as your action that turn (and vice versa)
- <span class="tag">Longer casting</span> spend action each turn; can't move; must maintain concentration
- <span class="tag">Spell targets</span> you must be able to see the target

## Concentration
Only one concentration spell at a time. When you **take damage**, make a **Constitution save**: DC = max(10, half damage taken). On a fail, the spell ends.

</div>
<div class="box" markdown="1">

## Sneaking & Stabbing
Attack an **undetected** target: attack with **adv.**. On hit vs Humanoid: Constitution save or knocked **unconscious**. Save DC modified by head protection.
*Rogues force the save with disadv..*

</div>
<div class="box" markdown="1">

## Resistances & Immunities
- <span class="tag">Resistance</span> halve damage (applied after all other modifiers).
- <span class="tag">Immunity</span> no damage from that type.
- <span class="tag">Vulnerability</span> double damage.
*Multiple resistances to the same damage type still only count as one.*

</div>
<div class="box" markdown="1">

## Underwater Combat
- <span class="tag">Melee</span> disadv. unless weapon deals **piercing**
- <span class="tag">Ranged</span> auto-miss beyond normal range; disadv. within normal range (except crossbows & thrown weapons)
- <span class="tag">Fully immersed</span> resistance to **fire** damage

</div> <!-- END column -->

## Conditions

<div class="conditions-grid" markdown="1">

<div markdown="1">
<span class="tag">Blinded</span> - auto-fail sight checks; attacks against = adv.; your attacks = disadv.

<span class="tag">Charmed</span> - can't attack charmer; charmer has adv. on social checks vs you

<span class="tag">Deafened</span> - auto-fail hearing checks

<span class="tag">Frightened</span> - disadv. on checks & attacks while source is visible; can't willingly move closer

<span class="tag">Grappled</span> - speed 0; ends if grappler is incapacitated or you are moved out of reach

<span class="tag">Incapacitated</span> - no actions or reactions

</div>

<div markdown="1">

<span class="tag">Invisible</span> - impossible to see without special sense; attacks against = disadv.; your attacks = adv.

<span class="tag">Paralyzed</span> - incapacitated, can't move or speak; auto-fail STR/DEX saves; attacks against = adv.; hits within 5 ft = critical

<span class="tag">Petrified</span> - transformed to solid, incapacitated; resistance to all damage; immune to poison & disease; auto-fail STR/DEX saves

<span class="tag">Poisoned</span> - disadv. on attack rolls and ability checks

<span class="tag">Prone</span> - melee = adv.; ranged = disadv.; your attacks = disadv.; stand up costs ½ speed
</div>

<div markdown="1">

<span class="tag">Restrained</span> - Speed 0; attacks against = adv.; your attacks = disadv.; disadv. on Dex saves

<span class="tag">Stunned</span> - incapacitated; auto-fail STR/DEX saves; attacks against = adv.

<span class="tag">Unconscious</span> - incapacitated, prone, drop held items; auto-fail STR/DEX saves; attacks against = adv.; hits within 5 ft = critical

<span class="tag">Exhaustion</span> - 1: disadv. on checks; 2: ½ speed; 3: disadv. attacks & saves; 4: HP max halved; 5: speed 0; 6: death
</div>

## Usage Die

After using a consumable, roll its usage die. On a **1 or 2** the die shrinks one step.

<span class="tag">d20 → d12 → d10 → d8 → d6 → d4 → 0 </span>(1 last shot for ammo)

| Die | Avg uses |
|:---:|:--------:|
| d20 | 30 |
| d12 | 20 |
| d10 | 14 |
| d8 | 9 |
| d6 | 5 |
| d4 | 2 |</div>

</div>
<div class="box" markdown="1">

## Short Rest

**Post-combat:** spend 1 HD; use Healer's kit

**At camp:** spend any number of HD

*See your class description for more short rest benefits.*

## Long Rest

**Spells:** regain PB or half PB spell slots (rounded up) depending on class. You regain your lowest spell slots first.

**Attune:** switch attuned magic items (max 3).

**The Long Rest:** gain short rest benefits, then choose **one:**
- Regain up to ½ max HP
- Regain up to ½ max HD
- Remove 1 exhaustion level
- Remove 1 negative condition
- Restore 1 reduced ability score
- Regain 1 inspiration (if < 3)
- Regain lowest spell slot

*See your class description for more long rest benefits.*

</div>

</div>

## Inspiration

Hold up to **3** points.

**Skill checks:** spend *after* seeing the result to reroll.

**Other rolls:** spend *before* the roll to gain adv..

</div>
<div class="box" markdown="1">

## Travel Roles

One leader per role; first helper grants adv., each further helper +1.

**Guide** - Int check at camp; sets navigation DC. Fail = half progress; fail by 5+ = no progress.

| DC | Terrain |
|:--:|:--------|
| 5 | Open plains, clear landmarks |
| 10 | Small hills |
| 15 | Light rain, woods |
| 20 | Heavy rain, forest |
| 25 | Fog, mountains |
| 30 | Night fog, magical terrain |

**Forager** - Wis check at end of day; success = 2d4 rations.

| DC | Terrain |
|:--:|:--------|
| 5 | Lush forest |
| 10 | Forest, coast |
| 15 | Thin woodland |
| 20 | Dry plains |
| 25 | Desert |
| 30 | Corrupted deadlands |

**Lookout** - Perception checks during travel. Fail = party is surprised.

</div>

## Travel Pace

| Pace | Hour | Day | Guide | Forage | Lookout |
|:-----|:----:|:---:|:-----:|:------:|:-------:|
| Slow | 1.5 mi | 12 mi | Adv. | Normal | Adv. |
| Medium | 2 mi | 16 mi | Normal | Disadv. | Normal |
| Fast | 3 mi | 24 mi | Disadv. | None | Disadv. |

| Pace | Hide Tracks | Effect |
|:-----|:-----------:|:-------|
| Slow | Adv. | Can use stealth |
| Medium | Normal | - |
| Fast | Disadv. | −5 to Perception |

**Forced March:** travel beyond 8 h/day. Each extra hour: Con save (DC 10+1/extra hour) or gain 1 exhaustion.

</div>
<div class="box" markdown="1">

## Supplies

**Food:** 1 lb per character per day. Can go without for CON mod + 3 days (min 1). Each day beyond that = 1 exhaustion.

**Water:** ½ gallon per day (1 gallon in hot weather). Less than required: DC 15 Con save or 1 exhaustion. Less than half: automatic 1 exhaustion.

</div>
<div class="box" markdown="1">

## Rest Activities

One primary activity per long rest, before sleep.

- <span class="tag">Cook Food</span> campfire + cooking tools; Cooking skill
- <span class="tag">Craft an Item</span> bandages, salves, arrows; materials + tools required
- <span class="tag">Maintain Gear</span> Gear maintenance kit (usage die d8)
  - *Daily Upkeep* - spend 1 kit use; first break on a proficient weapon or shield next day is prevented
  - *Sharpen Weapon* - Int check DC 10 (or Int Craft); success = reroll one damage die next day
  - *Maintain Armor* - Leatherworking or Metalsmith; success = temp HP equal to proficiency bonus
- <span class="tag">Repair an Item</span> Int (Craft) DC 10; success = item repaired

## Skill System

<div class="grid-2" markdown="1">
<div markdown="1">

**Getting skill points:** background events · class starting points · 1 Culture skill · +1 pt per level up · class features may grant extras

**Spending points** - must own the parent skill in the tree first:
- 1 pt per skill
- 2 pts → +1 ability score *(max ½ your level)*
- **Improved Proficiency** (*req. Expertise*) - 2 pts *(+1 cost after each time)* → +1 to all checks with that skill
- Ok to save skill points; spend after any long rest
</div>
<div markdown="1">

- **Expertise** - Advantage on checks with this skill
- **Expertise (X, Y)** - Adv.; one die uses mod X, the other mod Y
- **Group** - Choose one sub-skill; can be taken multiple times
- **Synergy (X)** - Advantage if you also have skill X
- **Trait** - Binary - no roll needed in most cases
- **Untrained** - Can attempt without proficiency
- **→ X** - Cost −1 if you have skill X

</div>

This group collects additional traits that are not part of a skill.

## Melee Weapon Properties

**Armor Piercer** - on a miss vs heavy armor, roll d4 and add to the attack roll.

**Chained** - swings around shields; +2 attack vs shield users.

**Close Quarters Combat** - no disadv. when prone; bonus action attack after escaping a grapple.

**Conceal** - bonus action Sleight of Hand vs Perception to draw; attack = advantage + 19–20 crit.

**Finesse** - use STR or DEX for attack and damage (same modifier for both).

**Shove** - replace an attack to shove up to two adjacent creatures (STR save or pushed 5 ft).

**Slam** - on crit or exceeding AC by 10+: target rolls next d20 with disadvantage.

**Thrown** - throw for a ranged attack using same ability mod; normal 20 ft / max 60 ft.

**Versatile** - use two hands to increase damage die one size.

</div>
<div class="box" markdown="1">

## Special Weapons

**Improvised** - 1d4 damage; thrown 20 ft / 60 ft; similar objects treated as real weapons.

## Ranged Weapon Properties

**Ammunition** - drawing ammo requires two hands; recover ½ after battle.

**Conceal** - Sleight of Hand vs Perception; attack = advantage.

**Heavy** - Small creatures have disadvantage.

**Loading** - spend one attack or bonus action to reload between shots.

**Range X/Y** - beyond X = disadvantage; beyond Y = can't attack.

**Net** - restrains Large or smaller until freed (DC 10 STR or 5 slashing damage to AC 10 net).

**Composite Longbow** - STR 14 or lower = disadvantage; may take attack penalty equal to STR mod to add STR mod to damage.

## Armor Rules

**Don / Doff**

| Category | Don | Doff |
|:---------|:----|:-----|
| Light | 1 min | 1 min |
| Medium | 5 min | 1 min |
| Heavy | 10 min | 5 min |
| Shield | 1 action | 1 action |

*With help, doffing time is halved.*

**Sleeping in armor** - light: no penalty; medium or heavy: regain only ¼ HP; exhaustion levels don't reduce.

**Pavise** - action to place/pick up; when placed gives half/¾ cover (prone) from one direction; no shield bonus while placed.

**Tower shield** - special Dodge: not lost even if incapacitated or speed 0.

**Armor spikes** (+25 gp) - successful grapple deals 1d3 piercing damage.

**Reinforced gorget** (+4 gp) - immunity to garrote special mechanics.
