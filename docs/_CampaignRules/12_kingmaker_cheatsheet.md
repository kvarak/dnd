# Kingmaker Cheat Sheet

These rules extend the standard Varlyn ruleset for the **Kingmaker** campaign. The party explores the Stolen Lands hex by hex on a regional map - each hex is 12 miles across. The day is divided into **Hexploration Activities (HAs)** spent on traveling, scouting, or camp tasks.

*These rules are provisional and may be revised or incorporated into the core Varlyn ruleset in the future.*

Other cheatsheets: [Varlyn Master Cheatsheet](../ResourcesTools/cheatsheet.html)

## Hexploration

Each day has a number of **Hexploration Activities (HAs)** based on pace.

| Pace | HA | Guide | Forage | Lookout |
|:-----|:--:|:-----:|:------:|:-------:|
| Careful | 1 | Adv. | Normal | Adv. |
| Normal | 2 | Normal | Disadv. | Normal |
| Reckless | 3 | Disadv. | None | Disadv. |

**Terrain cost to Travel or Reconnoiter a hex:**

| Terrain | HA |
|:--------|:--:|
| Open (plains, grasslands) | 1 |
| Difficult (forest, desert) | 2 |
| Greater difficult (mountain, swamp) | 3 |
| Road | −1 |

**HA modifiers:**
- <span class="tag">Exhaustion</span> −1 HA per 2 exhaustion levels
- <span class="tag">Forced March</span> +1 HA, travel only; Con save end of day (DC 10+5/day) or gain exhaustion
- <span class="tag">Severe Weather</span> −1 HA
- <span class="tag">Pathfinding / Mountain Routefinding</span> +1 HA (one skill only)

*Individual activities:* **Fortify Camp** (Int Craft vs terrain DC) · **Map the Area** (Investigation or Survival; requires Reconnoiter first)

## Weather Saves

While traveling in bad weather, each character makes a **Con save** per HA spent on Travel or gain 1 exhaustion.

| Weather | DC | Perception |
|:--------|:--:|:----------:|
| Light Rain / Snow | 5 | −1 |
| Heavy Rain / Snow | 10 | −2 |
| Hailstorm | 15 | −4 |
| Blizzard | 20 | −6 |
| Sandstorm / Tornado | 25 | −8 |

*Predict weather: Wis (Survival) DC 15 at Breaking Camp. Expertise with terrain skill.*

## Camp Activities

| Activity | Check |
|:---------|:------|
| Prepare Camp | Survival vs Zone DC |
| Hunting | Survival; 1 ration (+1 per 5 above DC) |
| Cooking | Cooking; no roll for basic meal |
| Camouflage | Survival or Stealth |
| Stand Guard | Perception; skip your camp activity |
| Subsist | Survival or social; −5 after exploring |
| Aid | First helper = adv.; each further = +1 |
| Other | Craft Item, Maintain Gear, Repair, etc. |
